# PERIX destination compass

`perix-compass.png` is a **168 × 168 pixel mechanical crop** of the supplied
original `perix-wordmark.png`, rectangle `(642, 0, 810, 168)`.

No redraw, generated artwork, palette substitution or tint was applied.
Used only for the explicitly selected destination on the existing map, at38px.
The surrounding original wordmark and PWA assets are unchanged by this crop.

`perix-compass-transparent.png` is the map version. Generated deterministically
with `scripts/transparent-compass.py`: flood-fill removal of the exterior white
background only. The enclosed dial, original colours, crop and dimensions are
preserved. The original `perix-compass.png` is not overwritten or redrawn.
